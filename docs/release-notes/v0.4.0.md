# Release notes — v0.4.0

Living Genie's fourth release closes two account-security gaps: **mandatory email verification**
on signup, and a self-service **forgot/reset password** flow. It also makes your language
preference part of your account rather than just your browser, so verification and reset emails
arrive in the right language from the start.

## What's new

- **Email verification** — after you register, we email you a one-time code; your account can't
  log in until you enter it, and entering a valid code verifies your account and signs you in
  right away.
- **Forgot/reset password** — request a reset code by email and use it to set a new password,
  without needing support. Resetting your password signs you out of every other device or browser
  that was logged into your account.
- **Account-level language preference** — the language you're using when you register, or switch
  to later while signed in, is now saved to your account, not just remembered by your browser.
- **Localized email** — verification and password-reset emails are written in your account's
  saved language.
- **Configurable SMTP** — self-hosters can point Living Genie at any SMTP-compatible provider
  purely through environment variables; local development still runs entirely via
  `docker compose up`, catching outgoing email locally instead of sending it for real.

## Not included yet

IP-based rate limiting of resend/reset requests (only a simple per-account minimum interval is
enforced for now), CAPTCHA on registration or forgot-password, two-factor authentication, and
changing or re-verifying an email address after registration are not part of v0.4.0. Login itself
still has no brute-force/account-lockout protection — that's separate from the new limit on
incorrect verification/reset code attempts, which is in scope.

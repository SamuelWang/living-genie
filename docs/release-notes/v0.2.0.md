# Release notes — v0.2.0

Living Genie's second release adds its first AI capability: **Genie**, a chatbot that answers
questions grounded in your own diary entries — with sources you can trace back to the entry it
drew from.

## What's new

- **Genie chat** — start a conversation and ask questions about your diary; Genie retrieves
  relevant excerpts from your own entries and answers based on them, citing which entries it drew
  from. If nothing relevant is found, it says so instead of making something up.
- **Automatic indexing** — new and edited diary entries are indexed for retrieval in the
  background, without slowing down saving; deleting an entry removes it from what Genie can
  reference.
- **Conversation history** — your conversations are saved: list them by most recent activity,
  reopen and continue one with earlier turns as context, or delete one (with a confirmation step)
  when you're done with it.
- **Streaming replies** — answers stream in as they're generated, with a clear loading indicator
  so the chat never feels frozen.
- **Bilingual replies** — ask in Traditional Chinese or English and Genie replies in the same
  language.
- **Stays on topic** — Genie only answers questions about your diary or about using Living Genie
  itself; it declines anything else rather than acting as a general-purpose assistant.
- **Still runs locally via Docker** — `docker compose up` brings up the full stack, including the
  new indexing pipeline, vector store, and local LLM inference, alongside everything from v0.1.0.

## Not included yet

Genie only knows about your diary entries in this release — other data sources (like todos) are
planned for a future release, per [../roadmap.md](../roadmap.md). Editing or regenerating a past
reply, and exporting or sharing a conversation, are also not part of v0.2.0.

# Release notes — v0.3.0

Living Genie's third release adds its first non-diary data source: **todos**. You can manage them
directly in the app, and Genie now understands them too — it can answer questions about your todos
and, when you ask, create, update, complete, or delete them for you right from the conversation.

## What's new

- **Todo management** — create a todo with a title, optional description, and optional due date;
  view your full list (pending and completed together); edit it; toggle it complete with a single
  click; or delete it, with a confirmation step to prevent accidental loss.
- **Automatic indexing** — new, edited, and completed/uncompleted todos are indexed for retrieval
  in the background, without slowing down saving; deleting a todo removes it from what Genie can
  reference.
- **Genie knows your todos** — ask Genie things like "what's due this week?" or "have I already
  bought milk?" and it answers from your actual todos, citing which one(s) it drew from. A single
  conversation can mix diary and todo questions naturally. If nothing relevant is found, Genie says
  so instead of making something up.
- **Manage todos through chat** — ask Genie to create a todo and it does so right away. Ask it to
  update, complete, or delete one, and it confirms which todo and what action first — the change
  only happens after you agree — and its reply links to the affected todo so you can quickly verify
  the result.
- **Smaller images, same quality** — images you upload into a diary entry are now compressed
  losslessly on save (converted to WebP, or re-optimized in place for JPEGs), so they take up less
  space with no visible difference in quality.
- **Warmer, more natural replies** — Genie's answers vary more in phrasing and read less like a
  report, building on v0.2.0's existing anti-verbatim behavior.

## Not included yet

Due-date reminders or notifications, recurring todos, and todo metadata beyond title/description/
due date/completed (priority, tags, subtasks) are not part of v0.3.0. A confirmed chat action (like
a confirmed delete) can't be undone, and there's no confirm/cancel button in chat — confirming an
action is done conversationally, by replying to Genie. Compression only applies to newly uploaded
images; images uploaded under earlier releases are unaffected.
